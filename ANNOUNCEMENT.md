I made PETSCII / 64 — a free Linux music visualiser with PETSCII-inspired graphics
and the C64's 16-colour palette.

It works with your existing music player: cliamp, Spotify, a browser, whatever
you're listening to. Three modes: a kaleidoscope, pulsing stereo diamonds, and
a mirrored character spectrum. Fullscreen, colour cycling, and trails included.

Try it, grab the code, or suggest a new effect:
https://github.com/caderbyshire/petscii-64

Tested on Omarchy / Arch Linux. MIT licensed. Feedback welcome!
